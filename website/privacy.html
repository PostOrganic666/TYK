Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026 · Applies to Toddler Mode for Mac and Toddler Mode for iPhone

The short version: Toddler Mode collects nothing. No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no tracking, no servers. Everything the app does happens on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and no server of ours to send anything to. We never see your data, your photos, or even how many people use the app.

Photos (iPhone app)

The iPhone app can display photos from your photo library in its kid-safe slideshow and gallery modes. Photos are read and displayed on your device only — they are never uploaded, copied off the device, or shared anywhere. The app contains no sharing, editing, or deleting functionality at all. Which photos are shown is controlled entirely by you in Settings.

Google Photos (optional, iPhone app)

If you choose to connect Google Photos, you sign in through Google's own sign-in page — the app never sees your password. You then pick photos in Google's picker, and the app downloads those specific picks to your device for offline display. Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's Keychain and can be removed at any time with the Disconnect button, which also lets you delete every imported photo. Nothing from Google Photos ever leaves your device again.

PIN and passwords

The optional parent PIN (iPhone) and unlock password (Mac) are stored only in the device's encrypted Keychain. We never receive them.

Children

Toddler Mode is a parent utility: a parent installs and configures it, then hands the device to their child inside a locked play session. The apps collect no personal information from anyone — child or adult.

Feedback

If you use the feedback form on our website, the message and optional email you type are delivered to us by our form provider so we can reply. That's the only time we receive anything from you, and it's entirely opt-in.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date. Given that the apps are built to collect nothing, don't expect much news here.

Contact

Questions? Use the feedback form on the home page.
